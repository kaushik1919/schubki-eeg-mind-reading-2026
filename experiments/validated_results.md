All development measurements use only `sub-01` through `sub-04` test labels. Hidden-subject EEG rows (`sub-05`, `sub-06`) were never used for model selection, and their row order is preserved in the final arrays.

The final submission files are regenerated by `scripts/reproduce_selected_submissions.sh` from the saved subject-level score matrices. The ensemble normalizes every score row before the weighted sum, avoiding scale dominance between feature families.
